What is a Data Diode?

A data diode is a hardware-enforced, one-way data transfer device that allows information to flow in a single direction only — making it physically impossible for data to travel in reverse. Used by governments, defense agencies, and critical infrastructure operators worldwide, data diodes are the gold standard for protecting air-gapped and segmented networks from cyber intrusion.
